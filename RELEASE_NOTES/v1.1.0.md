Meeting Minutes 1.1.0 adds Snapshots.

## Snapshots

- Take a Snapshot of part of your screen while a Recording runs, with a global hotkey or from the new floating Capture bar. Each Snapshot appears in the Transcript at the moment you took it.
- Drag a Capture region once and reuse it for later Snapshots, or draw a new one at any time. The first Snapshot asks for a region.
- Take Snapshots while a Recording is paused. They sit at the pause Marker in the order you took them.
- Open a Snapshot at full size, add or edit a caption, reveal it in its folder, or delete it and undo the deletion.
- Library search finds Snapshot captions and jumps to the nearest Segment.
- Snapshots are saved as PNG files in the Meeting folder at native resolution, survive a crash with the rest of the Recording, and are included when you export a Meeting.

## Capture bar

- An always-on-top, draggable, collapsible bar with pause, stop, Snapshot and a timer, shown only while a Recording runs.
- The Capture bar and the region overlay are excluded from Snapshots and from screen sharing.

## Settings and privacy

- Change the two Snapshot hotkeys in Settings (defaults Ctrl+Alt+S and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S). The app tells you when a hotkey cannot be registered.
- Snapshots stay on your PC. Summary, Ask and Citations do not read them, and nothing is sent to a transcription or language model provider.
- The Recording notice now says that Snapshots may capture content others share.

## Fixes

- Fixed the app freezing when taking a Snapshot from the Capture bar.
- Fixed Capture bar and region overlay chrome on Windows.
