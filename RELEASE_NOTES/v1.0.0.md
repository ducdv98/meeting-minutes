Meeting Minutes 1.0.0 is the first release.

## Record and keep Meetings

- Record Microphone and System audio from any Windows call app, or Import an existing audio file.
- Keep each Meeting in the local Library as readable audio and Transcript files.

## Turn audio into a Transcript

- Transcribe locally with Whisper, or use your own Groq or Gemini key for cloud transcription.
- Choose a Meeting language from 11 supported languages, or use Auto-detect.
- Re-transcribe with one chosen Speech-to-text model when needed.

## Understand and Ask

- Generate a Summary and Action items with Citations to Transcript Segments.
- Ask questions about one Meeting and follow Citations back to the audio.

## Optional Managed transcription

- Request a hand-issued Managed key to try experimental Managed transcription with a monthly allowance.
- The app has no telemetry. Your Library remains on your PC; the Privacy tab shows where data can leave it.
