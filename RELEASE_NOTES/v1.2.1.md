Meeting Minutes 1.2.1 updates the Managed transcription privacy text.

## Privacy

- Managed transcription is now described as an experimental free service. The Managed key notice and the Privacy tab say that the transcription gateway can use different speech-to-text providers, cloud or on our own infrastructure, and that saving a key also means agreeing to the gateway's policy, including how information is shared with those providers.
- Nothing about what the app sends changes. Managed transcription still starts only after you save a Managed key, and your own Groq, Gemini, OpenAI and Anthropic keys work as before.
