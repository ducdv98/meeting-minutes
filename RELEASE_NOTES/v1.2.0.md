Meeting Minutes 1.2.0 helps you never miss a call.

## Record prompt

- When another app starts using your Microphone and no Recording runs, a small always-on-top prompt names that app and offers **Record**, **Not now** and **Never for this app**. It appears once each time that app starts using the Microphone, and closes by itself after about 20 seconds.
- **Record** starts a Recording exactly like the main window's Record button, including the Recording notice the first time. The prompt never starts a Recording on its own.
- It works while the main window is closed and the app sits in the tray, and it stays out of screen sharing and Snapshots, like the Capture bar.
- Turn the prompt off, or remove apps you silenced, in Settings.

## Source health

- While a Recording runs, the app warns you when the Microphone has delivered no signal at all for 60 seconds, or when System audio has been silent for 3 minutes while your Microphone heard speech. Quiet rooms, waiting rooms and Voice notes do not set it off.
- The warning shows on the Capture bar and in the main window's recording bar, and Windows shows a notification. It names the Source and its device.
- **Open sound settings** opens Windows Sound settings; the Recording follows a change of the default device. **Dismiss** hides a warning for this Recording until the Source recovers and fails again.
- A warning never stops or pauses the Recording, and it clears by itself once the Source carries sound again.

## Settings and privacy

- The Record prompt is on by default. It reads only which apps are using the Microphone, on your PC, and sends nothing anywhere.
- Diagnostics count Source health warnings and Record prompts shown, without app names, devices or audio.
