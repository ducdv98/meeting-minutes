Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026 · Applies to Meeting Minutes for Windows

In short

To offer a Recording, the app checks locally which apps use the Microphone. Nothing about that check is sent anywhere. A Record prompt only offers to start; the app records only when you press Record. You can turn the prompt off or silence individual apps in Settings.

Snapshots are images you take manually during a Recording. Snapshots never leave your PC: the app does not send their images to transcription or language model providers, and Summary and Ask do not read them.

Your Meetings are stored in a Library on your PC. You can request a Managed key by email for experimental Managed transcription. Meeting Minutes has no app telemetry, analytics or ads. We do not sell your data. Processing runs locally by default. When you save a Managed key in Settings, audio can go to the Meeting Minutes transcription gateway, which can process it with different speech-to-text providers. If you add your own Groq key, audio goes to Groq for transcription; Gemini can receive audio for fallback transcription and Transcript text for Correction when you add a Gemini key. Saving a Managed key is your consent to cloud transcription; no audio is uploaded through Managed transcription before you save it. The app shows the Managed cloud privacy notice beside the key field and a one-time cloud notice when you save your first Groq, Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic key. Gemini's free tier may use content for training. For a cloud Summary, Action items or Ask with Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic, the app previews what it will send the first time you use that provider for a Meeting, and later sends still need your click. Your provider account handles those requests.

Diagnostics and your clipboard

When you choose Copy diagnostics, the app prepares a local report for your clipboard. It contains the app and Windows versions, CPU, RAM, audio device names, selected transcription model, provider type, audio duration by transcription provider and recent filtered log lines. Managed upload failures can include the service error code and request ID. It excludes Transcript, Summary and Ask text and API keys. Nothing is sent to us unless you choose to paste it into an email or issue.

The report includes counts of Record prompts shown and Source health warnings during this app session. These counts contain no app names, device names or audio content.

Key requests and Managed transcription

The Request a key form sends your email address and your description of how you will use Managed transcription to a private Google Sheet that only we can open, and emails us a copy. Google processes that data under its privacy policy. The page loads no Google script; your browser sends the form directly to Google when you press Send request. If sending fails, the form opens an email draft addressed to us instead, and your email provider handles that message under its terms. We use the information to review your request and respond, if we can. Keys are issued by hand during this experiment; we do not promise a response or turnaround time.

A Managed key grants access to the Meeting Minutes transcription service and carries a monthly usage allowance measured in Processed audio minutes. The service uses the key to authenticate and meter requests, keeps the key and usage counters, and reports remaining minutes when available. The allowance resets on the first day of each month (UTC). The service does not keep Meeting content long term: audio is deleted after processing, and each part's transcribed text is deleted within 10 minutes after processing. Saving the key in Settings is your consent to sending audio for Managed transcription.

Managed transcription is an experimental free service. By using a Managed key you also agree to the policy of our transcription gateway, including how information is shared with the speech-to-text providers it uses, as with the free tier of other services. The gateway can use several providers, cloud and local or built-in models, and they can change. We will describe the gateway's privacy in detail once it is stable and we offer paid plans.

Other software and this website

Microsoft Edge WebView2 renders the Windows app. WebView2 can collect required diagnostic data and, depending on your Windows diagnostic settings, optional data under Microsoft's WebView2 privacy terms. Meeting Minutes does not control that Windows component. The installer and app updates are served through GitHub Releases; transcription models come from Hugging Face.

This website is served by Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes visitor IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect the site; see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. We run no analytics and set no tracking or advertising cookies, and the pages load no third-party scripts. Fonts are served from this site. Cloudflare can set a strictly necessary security cookie if it needs to challenge a suspicious visitor.

Recording other people

You are responsible for telling participants that you are recording and for following the consent rules where you and they are. The app shows a Recording notice before the first Recording and an always-visible indicator in the window and tray while a Recording runs.

Delete or keep your data

Deleting a Meeting in the app moves its Library folder to the Windows Recycle Bin; empty the Recycle Bin to remove it permanently. Uninstalling keeps the Library. The uninstaller offers an unticked option to remove app data, including models, settings, the search index and logs. Remove your Managed key and other provider keys in Settings before uninstalling. To ask us to delete a key or your request email, contact us at the address below. You can also delete the Library, %APPDATA%\app.meeting-minutes.tech and %LOCALAPPDATA%\app.meeting-minutes.tech yourself when the app is closed.
