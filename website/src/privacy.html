Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026 · Applies to Meeting Minutes 0.1.0 for Windows

In short

Your Meetings are stored in a Library on your PC. Meeting Minutes currently has no Account, app telemetry, analytics or ads. We do not sell your data. Processing runs locally by default. If you connect experimental Managed transcription with an Invite code from the operator, audio goes to the Meeting Minutes service, which forwards it to Groq. If you add your own Groq key, audio goes to Groq for transcription; Gemini can receive audio for fallback transcription and Transcript text for Correction when you add a Gemini key. Connecting Managed transcription is your consent to cloud transcription; the app shows a one-time cloud notice when you save your first provider key. Gemini's free tier may use content for training. For a cloud Summary, Action items or Ask with Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic, the app previews what it will send the first time you use that provider for a Meeting, and later sends still need your click. Your provider account handles those requests.

Choosing Disconnect deletes the Managed credential from Windows Credential Manager. If the operator revokes access, the app deletes that credential when a token refresh is rejected. Processing then uses your own cloud keys or local Whisper.

Internet services see ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Model and update file downloads may use the providers' file hosts. The update manifest supplies the package URL; the app checks its signature before installing it.

Diagnostics and your clipboard

When you choose Copy diagnostics, the app prepares a local report for your clipboard. It contains the app and Windows versions, CPU, RAM, audio device names, selected transcription model, provider type, audio duration by transcription provider and recent filtered log lines. Managed upload failures can include the service error code and request ID. It excludes Transcript, Summary and Ask text, API keys, Invite codes and Managed tokens. Nothing is sent to us unless you choose to paste it into an email or issue.

Planned: Accounts

Account registration is planned and is not available in the current app. Invite codes will be retired when Accounts launch. Sign-in will use a verified email address; the service will keep the email address and usage counters, but not Meeting content long term. Audio will be deleted after processing, and each part's transcribed text may be kept for up to five minutes for retries. Signing in alone will not enable Managed transcription; you will choose to enable it separately.

Other software and this website

Microsoft Edge WebView2 renders the Windows app. WebView2 can collect required diagnostic data and, depending on your Windows diagnostic settings, optional data under Microsoft's WebView2 privacy terms. Meeting Minutes does not control that Windows component. The installer and app updates are served through GitHub Releases; transcription models come from Hugging Face.

This website is served by Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes visitor IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect the site; see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. We run no analytics and set no tracking or advertising cookies, and the pages load no third-party scripts. Fonts are served from this site. Cloudflare can set a strictly necessary security cookie if it needs to challenge a suspicious visitor.

Recording other people

You are responsible for telling participants that you are recording and for following the consent rules where you and they are. The app shows a Recording notice before the first Recording and an always-visible indicator in the window and tray while a Recording runs.

Delete or keep your data

Deleting a Meeting in the app moves its Library folder to the Windows Recycle Bin; empty the Recycle Bin to remove it permanently. Uninstalling keeps the Library. The uninstaller offers an unticked option to remove app data, including models, settings, the search index and logs. API keys and the Managed credential are separate in Windows Credential Manager; remove them in Settings before uninstalling. You can also delete the Library, %APPDATA%\app.meeting-minutes.tech and %LOCALAPPDATA%\app.meeting-minutes.tech yourself when the app is closed.
