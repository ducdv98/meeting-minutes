Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026 · Applies to Meeting Minutes 0.1.0 (Beta) for Windows

In short

Your Meetings are stored on your PC. Meeting Minutes needs no account and has no app telemetry, analytics or ads. We do not sell your data. Recording and transcription run locally. Meeting content leaves your PC only when you choose a cloud Summary or Ask using your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key. The first time you use each provider for a Meeting, the app shows the Transcript text and instructions it plans to send; consent is remembered for that Meeting and provider, and every later send still needs your click. Audio files are not sent to these providers.

Diagnostics and your clipboard

When you choose Copy diagnostics, the app prepares a local report for your clipboard. It contains the app and Windows versions, CPU, RAM, audio device names, selected transcription model, provider type and recent filtered log lines. It excludes Transcript, Summary and Ask text and API keys. Nothing is sent to us unless you choose to paste it into an email or issue.

Other software and this website

Microsoft Edge WebView2 renders the Windows app. WebView2 can collect required diagnostic data and, depending on your Windows diagnostic settings, optional data under Microsoft's WebView2 privacy terms. Meeting Minutes does not control that Windows component. The installer and app updates are served through GitHub Releases; transcription models come from Hugging Face.

This website is served by Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes visitor IP addresses and request details to deliver and protect the site; see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. We run no analytics and set no tracking or advertising cookies, and the pages load no third-party scripts. Fonts are served from this site. Cloudflare can set a strictly necessary security cookie if it needs to challenge a suspicious visitor.

Recording other people

You are responsible for telling participants that you are recording and for following the consent rules where you and they are. The app shows a Recording notice before the first Recording and an always-visible indicator in the window and tray while a Recording runs.

Delete or keep your data

Deleting a Meeting in the app moves its Library folder to the Windows Recycle Bin; empty the Recycle Bin to remove it permanently. Uninstalling keeps the Library. The uninstaller offers an unticked option to remove app data, including models, settings, the search index and logs. API keys are separate in Windows Credential Manager; remove them in Settings before uninstalling. You can also delete the Library, %APPDATA%\app.meeting-minutes.tech and %LOCALAPPDATA%\app.meeting-minutes.tech yourself when the app is closed.
