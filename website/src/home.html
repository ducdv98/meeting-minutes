Private by default, in plain words

Your Library stays on your PC

Your Meeting files stay in your Library. With your keys, Groq can receive audio for transcription; Gemini can receive audio for fallback transcription or Transcript text for Correction.

Your own cloud keys

Summary, Action items and Ask can use Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic. The app asks before sending a Meeting. Gemini's free tier may use content for training.

Nothing about you

No account or telemetry. The app checks for updates and downloads models when you choose one.

Works with any call app

It records what your PC plays and what your Microphone hears. Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and other apps that make sound work without an integration.
