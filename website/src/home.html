Private by default, in plain words

Your Library stays on your PC

Your Meeting files stay in your Library as plain files. The Privacy tab in the app shows where data can leave your PC. With your keys, Groq can receive audio for transcription; Gemini can receive audio for fallback transcription or Transcript text for Correction.

Your own cloud keys

Summary, Action items and Ask can use Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic. The app asks before sending a Meeting. Gemini's free tier may use content for training.

Managed transcription is experimental

You can request a Managed key, which is issued by hand. Saving it in Settings allows audio to go to the Meeting Minutes service. The app has no telemetry. Request a key.

The Record prompt appears when another app starts using your Microphone. It names the app and offers to record, but only starts when you press Record. You can turn it off or silence individual apps in Settings. The app checks Microphone use locally and sends nothing about that check.

Source health warnings appear during a Recording if the Microphone has no signal or System audio stays silent while you speak. The Capture bar and main window name the affected Source so you can check it without stopping the Recording.
