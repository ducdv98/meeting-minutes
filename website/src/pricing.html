How the allowance works

The allowance is measured in Processed audio minutes. Each Source counts separately, so a Meeting with Microphone and System audio uses minutes for both. An Import counts as one stream. Re-transcribing the same audio counts again.

The Managed transcription allowance resets on the first of each month (UTC), without rollover. It covers transcription only, not Correction, Summary or Ask.

When the allowance runs out or the service is unavailable, the app falls back to your own keys, then local Whisper.

Signing in alone does not turn on Managed transcription. You choose to enable it separately before audio is sent to the service.
