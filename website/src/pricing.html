Planned: how the allowance will work

The allowance will be measured in Processed audio minutes. Each Source will count separately. A 60-minute Meeting with Microphone and System audio could use up to 120 minutes. An Import will count as one stream. Re-transcribing the same audio will count again.

The Managed transcription allowance will reset on the first of each month (UTC), without rollover. It will cover transcription only, not Correction, Summary or Ask.

When the allowance runs out or the service is unavailable, the app will fall back to your own keys, then local Whisper.

Signing in alone will not turn on Managed transcription. You will choose to enable it separately before audio is sent to the service.
