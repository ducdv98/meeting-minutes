Include diagnostics

  1. In the app, open Help.
  2. Choose Copy diagnostics.
  3. Paste it into your message. It includes versions, devices and recent log lines, not Transcript text or keys.

Windows says it protected your PC

The Free Beta installer is unsigned, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Download the installer only through this site, then choose More info and Run anyway if you trust the file. Signing is planned before the app leaves Beta.

Uninstalling

Use Settings → Apps in Windows. Your Library in Documents\Meeting Minutes is kept. The uninstaller offers an unticked option to remove downloaded models, settings and the search index.

Windows 11 x64 is supported. Windows 10 may work, but is untested.
