Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026 · Applies to Meeting Minutes 0.1.0 for Windows

The short version

Your meetings stay on your PC. The app has no account and no analytics. It only connects to the internet for the four reasons listed below, and it sends meeting content only when you click.

What the app connects to

WhereWhenWhat it receives
Hugging FaceYou download a transcription modelA file request. No meeting data.
GitHubThe app checks for updatesA request for the latest version. No meeting data.
Ollama on your PCYou make a Summary or Ask, with Ollama chosenThe Transcript, without leaving your PC.
OpenAI or AnthropicYou click Summarise or Ask, with your own keyThe Transcript and your question, under your account with them.

What is stored, and where

  • Meetings (audio, Transcripts, Summaries, questions): Documents\Meeting Minutes.
  • Settings, models, search index and a log: the app's folder in AppData. The log never contains what was said.
  • API keys: Windows Credential Manager.

Recording other people

You are responsible for telling people you record them and for following the consent rules where you and they are.

Uninstalling

Use Settings → Apps in Windows. Your meetings in Documents\Meeting Minutes are always kept. Tick the box in the uninstaller if you also want to remove downloaded models and settings.
