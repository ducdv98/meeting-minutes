# Delete a Meeting to the Recycle Bin

Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: 04
Spec: ../spec.md

## What

Delete a Meeting from its view or the list with a confirm dialog: move its folder to the Windows Recycle Bin and remove it from the index and search. Not allowed while it is recording.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Deleted Meeting folder is in the Recycle Bin and restorable; after restoring it and rescanning, it is back in the Library.
- [ ] Deleting the Meeting that is currently recording is blocked.
